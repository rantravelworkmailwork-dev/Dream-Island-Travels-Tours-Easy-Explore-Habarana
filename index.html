About the Sigiriya Region

Heart of Sri Lanka’s Cultural Triangle

Located in the heart of Sri Lanka’s Cultural Triangle, the Sigiriya region is one of the island’s most captivating destinations.

Surrounded by Sigiriya, Dambulla, and Habarana, this remarkable area offers an ideal blend of ancient heritage, wildlife, nature, and authentic village experiences. Visitors can explore the world-famous Sigiriya Rock Fortress, a UNESCO World Heritage Site renowned for its ancient frescoes, landscaped gardens, and breathtaking panoramic views. Nearby, the Dambulla Cave Temple features impressive Buddhist cave shrines adorned with centuries-old murals and statues. Habarana is the perfect base for discovering the region’s natural beauty, including wildlife safaris, traditional village tours, scenic lakes, and encounters with Sri Lanka’s famous elephant populations. Together, these attractions create an unforgettable cultural and nature experience.
